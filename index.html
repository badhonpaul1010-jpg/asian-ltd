About Us

Your trusted partner for power & equipment on site

Asia Engineering Pte Ltd is a Singapore-registered company specialising in electrical works and the supply of electrical equipment and machinery to construction sites. From the first temporary power connection to final handover, we keep your project energised and moving.

We work closely with main contractors, sub-contractors and developers to provide reliable equipment, skilled manpower and responsive support — with safety and compliance at the heart of everything we do.

  • Temporary & permanent electrical installations
  • Supply & rental of site machinery and equipment
  • Workplace-safety focused team
  • Fast delivery across Singapore
